## Easy to confuse

- `VERIFIED_STATIC` `setting(GMD)` maps names and builds inputs. It does not convert the welfare variable to a different PPP year. `pppyear()` names the PPP vintage; `year()` names the survey-year variable. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_tables.ado:L103-L110`.

- `VERIFIED_STATIC` The Table 6 child call supplies `last3`. That means three cached observations; the Table 10 `within3` flag means a three-year window. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_tables.ado:L275-L275`.
