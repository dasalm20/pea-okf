## What it expects you to have ready

- `VERIFIED_STATIC` Load the variables named by the numeric options before the call. The brackets make these options syntactically optional; they do not guarantee a complete calculation when omitted. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figureC1.ado:L23-L23`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` The core report calls this producer through `pea core`; see the [pea_core record](004.md#s-f1c832ad1a080634-l1). Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_core.ado:L212-L221`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` Use national welfare and national poverty-line variables in matching units. Lines are renamed to `natline1`, `natline2`, etc. With `fgtvars`, the prepared `_fgt0_`, `_fgt1_` and `_fgt2_` fields must use those renamed line suffixes. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figureC1.ado:L71-L142`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` The forecast branch expects forecast years, poverty rates already in percent and GDP per person in constant local-currency units. Its list position pairs each forecast poverty series with a national line. P0-04 is fixed statically: every supplied forecast variable is counted for nonmissing values, independently. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figureC1.ado:L147-L181`; `Stata/plus/p/pea_figureC1.ado:L42-L53`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` Have `WDI_gdppc_lcuconst.dta` in PERSONAL `pea/`, keyed by `code year`. The `peb` path also needs `PEB_natpovrates.dta`; it can fill the first national poverty series. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figureC1.ado:L156-L165`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` Supply the intended observation weight. When no weight is supplied, the command creates a weight of 1. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figureC1.ado:L106-L112`.

## Easy to confuse

- `VERIFIED_STATIC` `comparability_peb()` names a text variable containing Yes/No, unlike the numeric comparability groups in other figures. This producer has no `comparability()` option. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figureC1.ado:L23-L40`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` `natpovlines()` contains poverty-line amounts; `natpov_fcast()` contains forecast poverty percentages. They are not interchangeable. This figure has no PPP welfare or PPP-year option. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figureC1.ado:L168-L181`; `Stata/plus/p/pea_figureC1.ado:L23-L23`.
