## pea_core

Welfare option: `natwelfare(natwelfare)` with `natpovlines(natline)`; `pppwelfare(welfppp)` with `ppppovlines(pline300 pline420 pline830)`; `onewelfare(welfppp)` with `oneline(pline300)`.

`VERIFIED_STATIC` `aggregate(groups)` selects regional and income groups, so it ignores the peer list. Forecast options are omitted because their known guard can stop Figure C1. confirm new file "pea_core.xlsx"

confirm numeric variable natwelfare natline
* GMD example conversion; use only with matching annual welfare and factors.

label values nowork pea_nowork
confirm string variable comparability_peb

pea_core [aw=weight_p], ///
    country(ISO3) benchmark(ISO3 ISO3 ISO3 ISO3) natwelfare(natwelfare) natpovlines(natline) ///
