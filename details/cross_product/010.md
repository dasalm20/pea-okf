## D03 — Paid work and industry have different eligible denominators

`VERIFIED_STATIC` C.1 rows 31–33 average nonmissing supplied employment/industry indicators across ages 15–64 without an explicit worker-only restriction. GMI employment/industry shares additionally require `lstatus==1`. The phrase “of which” does not establish an equal denominator.

**Resolved for GMD input, 0000-10-15.** `EXTERNAL_DEPENDENCY` The GMD harmonisation standard populates labour variables only for the employed, so `empstat` and `industrycat4` are missing for nonworkers by construction. The PEA package's absent `lstatus==1` is therefore redundant on GMD input and the two denominators cannot diverge. Source: confirmed by the GMD labour harmonisation maintainer on 0000-10-15; the standard itself lives outside both repositories and remains `EXTERNAL_DEPENDENCY`. Consistent with the observed a given country 0000 case, where `industrycat4` and `empstat` are nonmissing only for employed respondents.

`INFERRED` The difference stays latent, not absent. It would surface for non-GMD input, or for a GMD survey that violates the standard by recording an industry or employment type for a nonworker, for example from a last-job question. Treat a denominator check as required when either condition may hold.

PEA: [paid work](../stata_commands/038.md#s-844456a009101d88-l1), [agriculture](../stata_commands/038.md#s-a3bce7a98d8f1325-l1), `Stata/plus/p/pea_tableC1.ado:L615-L655`; `Stata/plus/p/pea_tableC1.ado:L663-L673`. ## D04 — Non-agriculture category bounds differ

`VERIFIED_STATIC` PEA's local `_nonagriculture` tests nonmissing industry unequal to 1. GMI `_industry_nonagr_ind` explicitly includes categories 2–4, with worker and age restrictions.

**Resolved for GMD input, 2026-10-15.** `EXTERNAL_DEPENDENCY` `industrycat4` carries exactly four categories under the GMD standard, so `~= 1` and `2–4` select the same records and the two expressions are numerically equivalent. This is a code-style difference, not a difference in result. Source: confirmed by the GMD labour harmonisation maintainer on 2026-10-15. `VERIFIED_STATIC` corroboration inside the repositories: the formatter labels exactly 1 Agriculture, 2 Industry, 3 Services, 4 Others (`01.Programs/02-GMI format.do:L312-L316`; `01.Programs/02-GMI format.do:L486-L491`), and GMI generates only `_industry_ind1` through `_industry_ind4`.

`INFERRED` The difference stays latent for non-GMD input or any source carrying a fifth industry code, where the PEA expression would admit records the GMI expression excludes. The worker and age restrictions noted in D03 are a separate matter and resolved there.

PEA: [C.1 row 33](../stata_commands/039.md#s-035945af52af261e-l1), `Stata/plus/p/pea_tableC1.ado:L615-L655`. GMI: [non-agriculture](../gmi_indicators/044.md#s-6184363b08079fa8-l1), `01.Programs/01ab-GMI extended indicators labor ind.do:L366-L366`.
