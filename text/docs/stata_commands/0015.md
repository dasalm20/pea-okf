## What it expects you to have ready

- `VERIFIED_STATIC` With the exact text `setting(GMD)`, the entry command runs `_pea_vars_set` and takes its variable mappings. The [helper contract](0004.md#L520-L568) lists the required raw fields and the generated not-working indicator. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figures.ado:L46-L52`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` Load the numeric welfare, poverty-line and survey-year variables before calling the command. `oneline()` and `onewelfare()` name one matched pair for single-line outputs; the national and PPP pairs serve different outputs. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figures.ado:L30-L150`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` When no weight is supplied, preparation uses a weight of 1. The workflow builds poverty and welfare intermediates for its children. [P1-01](../stata_errors/0004.md#L655-L672) records that child calls use analytic weights and do not forward `if`/`in`. Do not assume the original qualifier or weight type reaches every output. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figures.ado:L107-L150`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` Supply `welfaretype(INC)` or `welfaretype(CONS)` and a peer-year distance no greater than 10. The nonempty-type and distance checks happen before the figure calls; INC/CONS validation remains in Figure 9b/9c. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figures.ado:L32-L38`; `Stata/plus/p/pea_figures.ado:L55-L59`; `Stata/plus/p/pea_figure9b.ado:L48-L58`.

## Easy to confuse

- `VERIFIED_STATIC` `setting(GMD)` maps names and builds inputs. It does not convert the welfare variable to a different PPP year. `pppyear()` names the PPP vintage; `year()` names the survey-year variable. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figures.ado:L46-L52`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` `welfaretype()` describes income versus consumption for peer matching. `onewelfare()` names the actual welfare variable. They are not interchangeable. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figures.ado:L334-L347`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` A Figure 14 “Not done” line needs the [silent-failure checks](../stata_errors/0004.md#L6-L43); the summary line does not reveal the underlying child error. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figures.ado:L453-L462`.
