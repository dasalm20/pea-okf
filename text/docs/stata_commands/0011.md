## What it expects you to have ready

- `VERIFIED_STATIC` Load the variables named by the numeric options before the call. The brackets make these options syntactically optional; they do not guarantee a complete calculation when omitted. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figure6.ado:L22-L22`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` The report workflow produces this figure through `pea figures`; see the [pea_figures record](0014.md#L540-L557) for the entry options and preparation. The declaration here belongs to the individual producer. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figures.ado:L269-L269`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` Have observations for both years in each requested spell. Write pairs as `spells(0000 0000; 0000 0000)`: a space between the years and a semicolon plus a space between pairs. These are example years, not required dates. Evidence: `source-ref-98abd6b846add01bfcbe.do:L158-L158`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` With `fgtvars`, `_fgt0_<welfare>_<line>` must already exist for each selected welfare/line pair. It is 1 below the line and 0 otherwise, with missing values outside the usable sample. This is a prepared poverty indicator, not raw welfare. See [prepared fields](0004.md#L646-L692). Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figure6.ado:L134-L142`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` The poverty helper builds those fields only for nonmissing welfare and lines in the selected sample. Without `fgtvars`, pre-existing fields with the same names cause its creation check to stop. Evidence: `Stata/plus/_/_pea_gen_fgtvars.ado:L24-L48`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` Have a literal numeric `year` field and country-year GDP records in PERSONAL `pea/PIP_all_GDP.dta`. The command keeps and merges on literal `year`, even though it accepts `year()`. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figure6.ado:L141-L164`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` Supply the intended observation weight. When no weight is supplied, the command creates a weight of 1. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figure6.ado:L49-L55`.

## Easy to confuse

- `VERIFIED_STATIC` This command accepts `onewelfare()` with `oneline()` naming the matching poverty-line variable. The option takes a variable, not a numeric line value. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figure6.ado:L22-L22`.
- `VERIFIED_STATIC` The third series is the ratio of annual poverty growth to annual GDP growth. It is not a poverty percentage; a zero GDP-growth denominator cannot give a usable ratio. Evidence: `Stata/plus/p/pea_figure6.ado:L162-L176`.
